# Provenance and reuse

Package: mythicmobs-codex-kit 1.0.0. Research date: 2026-11-05, Asia/Bangkok.

The skill instructions, summaries, checker and deployable NXK_ templates were newly authored for this request. They are not official MythicCraft software and have no MythicCraft endorsement. Templates are based on documented interfaces and general design ideas, not redistributed commercial packs.

Community material is limited to brief attributed excerpts and observations in `references/patterns-and-community.md`. Original files are linked, not bundled. Their licenses and authors' expertise were not independently established. The historical addon example is deliberately non-deployable documentation.

Official references were inspected online; the kit stores concise notes and source metadata rather than copied manuals. Source IDs are stable inside this package. `sources.json` records inspected URLs, evidence type and limitations. URLs can change after the snapshot date.

No Minecraft server, MythicMobs JAR or commercial plugin license was available for testing. YAML validation, documentation comparison and reference checks are the evidence supplied; runtime certification is not claimed. Python and PyYAML are not included in the ZIP; the checker declares its small external dependency separately.
